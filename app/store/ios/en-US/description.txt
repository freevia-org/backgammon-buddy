Build your backgammon skills through play, review and practice.

Play against the built-in computer opponent or share one device with another player. Review completed games, explore engine evaluations and save useful positions for practice. Local play, analysis and learning work offline.

• Computer opponents and local two-player games
• On-screen hints and move explanations in supported solo modes
• Post-game review and a saved game archive
• Practice exercises and learning progress
• Match exports in SGF, MAT and XG formats
• Nearby play on trusted Wi-Fi, with optional QR joining
• Online play when the service is configured
• Themes, larger text and 28 interface languages

Live nearby and online matches are unassisted. Nearby traffic is unencrypted; use trusted Wi-Fi. Camera access is optional and used only to scan a nearby game's QR code. A manual address entry option is available.

This is a board game with no real-money wagering or prizes. Physical-board recognition, microphone listening and spoken Buddy coaching are not included in this version.
