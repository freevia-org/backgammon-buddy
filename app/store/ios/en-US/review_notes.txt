No account or login is required for local play, review or learning.

1. Open Play vs Computer from Home and start a game. Roll, select a legal move and confirm. Solo games offer tutoring; live multiplayer games do not.
2. Finish a game to inspect its review and archive entry. Save a reviewed position as an exercise and open Learning to practise it.
3. Settings contains Privacy, Diagnostics, feedback and third-party licenses. Diagnostics previews a report before opening a GitHub draft; opening a draft transmits the previewed details to GitHub. No issue is posted automatically.
4. Nearby play requires two devices on trusted Wi-Fi. Join supports manual host-address entry and optional camera QR scanning. Discovery is best-effort on iOS. Local-network access is requested in context; camera refusal leaves manual entry available.
5. Online uses Firebase anonymous authentication. Use Online to create/join with a second device. Anonymous identities are created only for online play. Settings > Privacy provides Delete online identity and data. Requests freeze access immediately and are processed within 30 days. After requesting deletion, use Settings > Privacy > Check deletion confirmation. The private receipt works after sign-out without creating another account. Keep this installation until confirmed; receipts remain available online for 90 days. A missing or expired receipt is not proof of deletion.

Physical-board Buddy Mode, microphone listening and spoken coaching are disabled in v1. No money, wagers or prizes are involved.
