AI Gammon's native wrapper is derived from wildbg's wildbg-c crate. The wrapper
adds wildbg_new_with_path to load neural nets from a runtime directory and
removes the exported wildbg_new constructor that embeds demo nets. These
changes are recorded in native/engine_shim/src/lib.rs in the app source.
