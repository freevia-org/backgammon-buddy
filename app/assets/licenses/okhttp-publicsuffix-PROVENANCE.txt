The source contains 9,499 normal/wildcard rules and 8 exceptions. Removing
comments, sorting unsigned UTF-8 bytes, splitting !-prefixed exceptions and
writing two big-endian length-prefixed newline-delimited lists reproduces
every uncompressed byte of okhttp3/internal/publicsuffix/publicsuffixes.gz
in the resolved OkHttp 4.12.0 JAR. This is the format used by upstream's
PublicSuffixListGenerator.java at the same release commit.
